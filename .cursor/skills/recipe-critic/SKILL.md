# Recipe Critic

Review every candidate before human approval.

1. Confirm the technique matches the dish identity.
2. Check that ingredient order, heat, doneness cues, and step timing are actionable.
3. Fail closed on declared allergens and absolute taboos.
4. Reject impossible time claims; in named-dish mode retain the recipe with an explicit warning.
5. Flag risky hot oil, undercooked poultry, shellfish, and storage steps with plain safety wording.
6. Require provenance and distinguish traditional reference, household adaptation, and generated inspiration.

Read [references/quality-checklist.md](references/quality-checklist.md) before approval.
