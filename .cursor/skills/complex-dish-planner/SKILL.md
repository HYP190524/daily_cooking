# Complex Dish Planner

Represent elapsed time honestly.

1. Separate active minutes, total elapsed minutes, and advance-prep minutes.
2. Detect soaking, marinating, stock-making, proofing, chilling, and resting.
3. Preserve ingredient-specific preparation and staged order.
4. When the user's time limit is impossible, keep the authentic schedule and emit a soft warning.
5. Offer a clearly named household adaptation only when it preserves the dish's recognizable identity.
6. Never turn a braise, stew, simmer, steam, fry, roast, or cold toss into generic stir-frying merely to fit the limit.

Read [references/complexity-rules.md](references/complexity-rules.md) for thresholds.
